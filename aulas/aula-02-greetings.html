Capítulo 1

Os 4 registros: como escolher o tom certo?

Em inglês, o registro é o tom que você usa dependendo da situação. Pense nele como a roupa adequada para cada ocasião — não se usa terno na praia nem bermuda numa reunião de diretoria.

📝 Teacher's Activity

Chapter 4 · Dialogue: Street Encounter

📋 Activity

Create a dialogue between two people who meet on the street and talk about their families. Use the greetings learned in class (formal and informal).

Characters: Alex and Jordan — old friends who run into each other on the street

🏙️ Scene: two people unexpectedly meet on the street
A
Hey, Jordan! Long time no see!
J
Alex! How are you doing today?
A
I'm great, thanks! What about you?
J
I'm good! Hey, how is your family?
A
They are great, thanks for asking! My mom and dad are healthy. My sister is busy — she is a teacher now!
J
Oh, really? That's wonderful! My brother is a student. He is at university.
A
Nice! And your parents? How are they?
J
They are fine. My mom is at home and my dad is at work.
A
Great! Your family is amazing.
J
Thank you! Your family is great too. Well, I have to go — see you!
A
See you later!

✅ Greetings used in the activity (Chapter 3)

"Hey!" → Informal greeting — perfect for a casual street encounter.
"Long time no see!" → Informal/playful greeting — signals they're old friends who haven't seen each other in a while.
"How are you doing today?" → Informal variation of Hello / How are you?

💡 Verb TO BE in the activity (Lesson 01)

The dialogue uses several forms of TO BE to talk about family: I am (I'm) · She is · He is · They are · My mom is · My dad is

🔑 Regra de ouro: mantenha o registro consistente

Uma vez que você identifica o contexto (formal ou informal), mantenha o mesmo tom do início ao fim. Misturar "How do you do?" com "Peace out!" na mesma conversa soa inconsistente e pode confundir o interlocutor.

⚠️ Os 3 erros mais comuns

  1. "Good night" como cumprimento → ❌ Nunca! É só despedida ou antes de dormir. Use "Good evening" para cumprimentar à noite.
  2. "How do you do?" com amigos → ❌ Extremamente formal. Resposta certa: repetir a pergunta, nunca "Fine, thanks!"
  3. Gírias ("What's up?", "Peace out!") em contexto formal → ❌ Fuja em entrevistas, reuniões e lojas elegantes.

✅ Checklist para escolher a expressão certa

  1. Identifique o contexto: formal ou casual?
  2. Pense em quem está do outro lado: colega próximo, cliente, amigo, chefe?
  3. Escolha o registro adequado (🎩 Formal / 😊 Neutro / 👋 Informal / 🎉 Playful).
  4. Lembre-se da resposta esperada para cada expressão.
  5. Mantenha o mesmo tom do início ao fim da conversa. 🎉
